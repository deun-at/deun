# presentation-file-split

## Spec
- Source: idea — code review 2026-10-22 (research/code-review-2026-10.md), Architecture item: the god files `claim_page.dart` (1631 lines) and `expense_entry_widget.dart` (1477 lines), plus CODE_REVIEW_2026-07-07 §6's "move the rebalancing math into `split_allocation.dart`"
- Goal: `claim_page.dart` and `expense_entry_widget.dart` split into section files, and the split editor's rebalancing math moves into `split_allocation.dart` with its own unit tests. Nothing changes for the user.
- Why: Jakob as sole maintainer · noticed by: the backend rebuilds from supabase/migrations/, the fallback code paths are gone, the 1500–1900-line files open in pieces

  - [auto] Exact-split rebalancing has its own unit tests. Given an entry total, the included members and the locked members, each included member who is not locked gets an equal part of (total minus the locked included amounts). Locked amounts and the amounts of members who are not included stay as they were. 12.00 with Alice locked at 9 gives Bob 3. 10.00 over three unlocked members gives 10/3 each, not cent-rounded parts, and `SplitAllocation.compute` reports the result as ok.
  - [auto] Percentage rebalancing has its own unit tests that work the same way against 100. With Alice locked at 50, two unlocked members get 25 each. Three unlocked members get 100/3 each, and `SplitAllocation.compute` reports the result as ok.
  - [auto] Edge cases never produce NaN or Infinity and change nothing. With no included members, every amount and percentage comes back unchanged. When every included member is locked, nothing is reassigned. Unit tests cover both.
  - [auto] Scaling a total moves the locked amounts with it, proven by unit tests. Each locked amount scales by new total ÷ old total (Alice locked at 9 of 12 becomes 18 of 24), and amounts of members who are not locked stay as they were. When either total is ≤ 0, or nobody is locked, the locks are released and every amount comes back unchanged.
  - [auto] The scaled split is what gets saved. In a quick split in Exact mode with two members and a 12.00 total, the user keypad-edits Alice to 9 and then changes the expense-level amount to 24.00. The form's `expense_entry[0][share_data]` must then be {a: 18, b: 6} and `expense_entry[0][locked_members]` must be {a}. Proven by a new widget test that is green at HEAD.
  - [auto] An unbalanced split still blocks the save. In a quick split, an Exact split whose amounts do not add up to the entry total fails validation with `splitAmountError`. Percentages that do not add up to 100 fail with `splitPercentageError`. A balanced split of either kind validates clean. Proven by a new widget test that is green at HEAD; no test covers this validator today.
  - [auto] `dart format`, `flutter analyze` and `flutter test` pass.

## Approach
- Work in four steps, in this order: characterize, move the math, split the entry widget, split the claim page. Every step leaves `flutter test` fully green, so a regression points at one step. The claim page split is close to cut-and-paste. Each private class is already a self-contained Stateless or Stateful widget (plus one `CustomPainter`) with explicit constructor parameters. None of them touches `ref` or `supabase`, and the file has no private top-level helpers. Moving one is a rename plus an import. The only stateful pieces are `_PresencePulse` (its animation controller) and `_SplitEditorCard` (seeded from `initialClaimers`), and they move unchanged.
- The entry widget is the real work. It is one State with thirteen widget-building `_build*` methods over shared mutable state. Extract stateless section widgets that receive values and callbacks. The State keeps the controllers (hand down a get-or-create callback), so disposal stays in one place. Moving a `FormBuilderField` into a child is safe, because fields register with their nearest `FormBuilder` ancestor and `_syncFormFields` looks them up by name. Keep the tree shape stable so no field remounts and loses its state.
- The math move is mechanical once the characterization tests stand. The State calls the pure functions and assigns the returned maps, and applies `releaseLocks` with `clear()` on its existing Set.
- Do not run this alongside scan-total-applies (it edits `_buildUnitPriceField`, `_openUnitPriceKeypad` and `didUpdateWidget` in this widget), helper-split (it moves helpers both files import), notification-context-free (it edits the claim nudge and turns `ClaimNotificationSender` into an alias), push-server-compose (it reshapes the claim nudge and `ClaimNotificationSender` again) or injectable-repositories (it touches the `supabase.auth` reads in both States). Whichever of each pair lands second rebases. The nudge handler stays in `_ClaimPageState` either way, so the nudge edits of notification-context-free and push-server-compose never reach a section file.
