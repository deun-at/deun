# realtime-channel-lifecycle — Realtime retries never outlive or duplicate a channel

## Spec
- Source: idea — code review 2026-10-22 (research/code-review-2026-10.md), finding 2 — the As-is lines tagged "for realtime-channel-lifecycle"
- Flight: review-bugs
- Goal: A realtime channel lives exactly as long as the notifier that opened it. A pending retry never outlives a dispose or doubles a channel on resume, and no channel callback lands in a disposed notifier.
- Why: group members using the app · noticed by: a failed save or delete no longer throws away the form, a retried payback no longer double-records, a joining guest no longer rewrites history outside the group

## Contract
- Acceptance:
  - [auto] **Disposed while a retry is pending.** A notifier's channel reports `channelError` (or `timedOut`) and the notifier is then disposed. When the backoff would have fired, no new channel opens, no error is raised and the realtime connection status stays as it was. Nothing is left scheduled to fire later.
  - [auto] **Resume, with or without a pending retry.** After an app resume (one `appResumeCounterProvider` bump), every config the notifier subscribed has exactly one live channel, and every channel it replaced is closed. That holds straight away when no retry was pending, and still holds once a pending retry's backoff has elapsed. A postgres event delivered afterwards reaches `onEvent` exactly once. A notifier with two configs (FriendshipListNotifier's shape) ends with two live channels, one per name.
  - [auto] **Repeated error reports.** A channel that reports an error more than once before its retry fires (`channelError` then `timedOut`, or `channelError` twice) gets one replacement channel after the backoff, not one per report.
  - [auto] **Superseded channels.** A status reported by a channel that was replaced (by a resume or a retry) or closed (by dispose) schedules no retry, opens no channel and leaves the connection status unchanged.
  - [auto] **Nothing gets through after dispose.** Once the notifier is disposed, a postgres event, a subscribe status or a presence change from any channel it opened calls neither `onEvent` nor `onPresence` and raises no error.
  - [auto] **A live notifier still recovers.** After a `channelError` on a mounted notifier, replacement channels open on the retry schedule ruled under Decisions (Retry policy). Once that budget is spent, the connection status goes to disconnected and nothing more is scheduled. A later `subscribed` status (for example after a resume) sets it back to connected.
  - [auto] **Presence keeps working.** A channel subscribed with a `presencePayload` tracks it exactly once after reporting `subscribed`. Its replacement after a resume or retry also tracks it exactly once, and a superseded channel never tracks it. `ClaimNotifier.claimingNow` equals the count its live claim channel last reported and ignores counts from replaced channels. How the claim header renders that count is already enforced by test/widgets/claim_page_test.dart ('header shows merchant and live claiming count', 'F164: live count derives from presence, not member-totals length' and 'F164: zero presence shows the "No one claiming yet" branch'), which must stay green.
  - [auto] **A fetch that outlives its notifier.** These notifiers can be disposed while a fetch is still in flight: ExpenseListNotifier (realtime insert/update, and the resume reload), GroupDetailNotifier (realtime event and resume reload) and ClaimNotifier (realtime refetch and resume refetch). When that fetch completes, it writes no state and raises no error.
  - [auto] **The mounted path is unchanged.** While ExpenseListNotifier is mounted, a realtime insert adds the fetched expense to its list, an update replaces the entry with the same id, and a delete removes it.
  - [auto] `dart format`, `flutter analyze` and `flutter test` pass.
  - [human] On a phone against the live instance, live updates and claim presence still arrive through the refactored channel code, both before and after the app is backgrounded and resumed.

- Consumes: —
- Decisions:
  - Scope -> finding 2 only: channel lifecycle in lib/helper/realtime_mixin.dart, plus the post-await guards below. Channel names, tables, filters and which events each notifier reacts to stay as they are; realtime-event-scope owns those (research/code-review-2026-10.md, As-is).
  - Pending retry vs dispose and resume -> `disposeChannels` cancels it. `resubscribeChannels` supersedes it: the retry is cancelled and the resubscribe opens the replacement with a fresh retry budget (Jakob in prep code-review-2026-10 (2026-10-24)).
  - Channels per config -> at most one live channel per config name at any time, and at most one pending retry per config. Names already embed the family argument (`expense_list:$groupId`, `claim:$expenseId`, …), so they are unique across notifiers too. Another error report from a channel whose retry is already pending is ignored, because realtime_client 2.7.1 `subscribe()` reports `channelError` from both its `_onError` hook and the join push's `error` reply, so one channel can report more than once (Jakob in prep code-review-2026-10 (2026-10-24); realtime_client 2.7.1 realtime_channel.dart in the pub cache).
  - Generic guard -> the mixin itself drops any callback (event, status, presence) from a channel that is no longer its config's live channel, whether it was superseded, retried away or disposed. The callback is dropped before it reaches the notifier, its Ref or the connection status (Jakob in prep code-review-2026-10 (2026-10-24)).
  - `disposeChannels()` at the top of every `build()` -> the call stays. Riverpod 3.2.1 keeps one notifier instance per provider across rebuilds (3.0.0-dev.16 reverted the dev.12 "recreated on rebuild" change; notifier_provider.dart creates it once via `classListenable.result ??=`), and it runs `ref.onDispose` callbacks before every rebuild as well as on dispose (element.dart `_performRebuild`). So the top-of-build call is redundant with the registered onDispose, but harmless. Because the same mixin instance subscribes again after its own `disposeChannels` on every rebuild, the guard is generation-based, not a one-way "disposed" flag (riverpod 3.2.1 CHANGELOG and source in the pub cache).
  - Retry policy -> unchanged from today's mixin (`_maxRetries = 3`, delay `1 << attempt` s): 3 retries at 1 s, 2 s and 4 s. The fourth failure in a row (the first attempt plus 3 retries) calls `markDisconnected` and schedules nothing, and `subscribed` calls `markConnected`. The budget belongs to one retry chain and resets only on a resubscribe or a new subscribe. Keeping it as-is keeps this a lifecycle fix (research/code-review-2026-10.md As-is, lib/helper/realtime_mixin.dart).
  - Retry mechanism -> one cancellable `Timer` per config replaces the untracked `Future.delayed`, which cannot be cancelled (research/code-review-2026-10.md As-is: "retry `Future.delayed` (142) is untracked").
  - Resubscribe coalescing -> `_isResubscribing` and its microtask reset stay as they are. With one slot per config, a second resubscribe would still leave one live channel per config, so the flag is neither needed nor harmful, and removing it is not lifecycle work (planning, from lib/helper/realtime_mixin.dart).
  - Seam -> all channel creation and removal goes through `RealtimeChannelFactory`. The mixin reads it from `realtimeChannelFactoryProvider` through the Ref passed to `subscribeToChannel`, once per subscribe, and holds it, so a retry never touches a Ref (Jakob in prep code-review-2026-10 (2026-10-24): "an injectable channel factory").
  - Seam location -> the interfaces, the adapter and the provider all live in lib/helper/realtime_mixin.dart, next to the mixin and its existing part file, so the five users' imports do not change (planning; research/code-review-2026-10.md lists the five mixin users).
  - Presence -> the adapter registers sync/join/leave and passes `onPresence` the distinct presence-key count (`presenceState().length`, as today). The mixin keeps "track `presencePayload` once after `subscribed`" and does it on each live channel. ClaimNotifier's call site does not change (Jakob in prep code-review-2026-10 (2026-10-24): claim_notifier.dart must keep working).
  - App pause -> unchanged. lib/navigation.dart still calls `supabase.removeAllChannels()` on pause and bumps `appResumeCounterProvider` on resume, and the mixin gets no pause hook. A retry that fires while the app is paused opens one tracked channel, and the resume replaces it (Jakob in prep code-review-2026-10 (2026-10-24)).
  - Post-await guard -> ExpenseListNotifier's `onEvent` re-checks `ref.mounted` after its await (Jakob in prep code-review-2026-10 (2026-10-24)). Planning widened this from the code: ExpenseListNotifier `reload`, GroupDetailNotifier `reload` (which its `onEvent` awaits) and ClaimNotifier's `onEvent` and resume refetch get the same re-check. research/code-review-2026-10.md calls these reloads "guarded" / "do check", but both check only before the await. ## Approach
- Land the seam first with no behaviour change, so the existing suite proves the move. Then replace the mixin's two parallel lists (`_channels`, `_configs`) with one live slot per config name: the config, its current handle, a generation counter, the retry attempt and at most one retry `Timer`. Each callback a handle is opened with captures its generation. It returns before touching the notifier, its Ref or the connection status unless the slot is still on that generation. That single check is the generic guard, and it is also what makes a superseded channel's late `channelError` harmless.
- How each operation uses the slot:
  - `disposeChannels` bumps every generation first, then cancels every timer, closes every handle and clears the slots, so a status a handle reports while closing is already stale.
  - `resubscribeChannels` bumps each slot's generation, cancels its timer and closes its handle the same way, but keeps the slot and reopens it at attempt 0.
  - An error on the live channel schedules a retry only if none is pending. The retry closes the channel and reopens it under a new generation.
- Tests run on `ProviderContainer.test` (precedent: test/provider/auth_switch_invalidation_test.dart, including `container.pump()`), with `realtimeChannelFactoryProvider` overridden by the fake and time under fake_async:
  - A test-only notifier that mixes in `RealtimeNotifierMixin` covers the mixin rules (criteria 1–7).
  - The three notifiers are overridden with subclasses whose fetch seams return `Completer` futures (criteria 7–9).
  - ClaimNotifier's build still reads `supabase.auth`, so its test initializes the dummy client the way test/widgets/claim_page_test.dart does.
- realtime-event-scope also edits mixin users (group_list, friendship_list) and extends the mixin, so it is not parallel with this feature. It depends on this feature (its Depends and Consumes): it builds on the slot-per-config mixin after this lands and reuses `FakeRealtimeChannelFactory`.
