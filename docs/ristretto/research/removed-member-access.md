## Why

Candidates (proposals — a person picks or rewrites):
- Members who remove someone · noticed by: the removed person can no longer read, edit or delete the group's expenses, rewrite its balances, delete the whole group, or put themselves back. Re-entry takes a fresh invite link from an active member, or an add by a member.
- The removed member · noticed by: a group they were removed from leaves their group list instead of sitting there fully editable, so they don't think they're still in it.

## Domain
- Soft vs hard removal: unsettled (|balance| ≥ the currency's epsilon) → blocked, nothing written. Settled with history (paid for any of the group's expenses, or holds a share on one) → `removed_at` stamped, row kept. Settled with no history → row deleted. · source: docs/ristretto/plans/group-member-removal.md Decisions; 20261026140000 `remove_group_member`, 2026-10-26 · proposed → domain/glossary.md
- What soft removal must keep: shares and ledger math stay unchanged. "Removal changes visibility, never ledger math". The removed member stays on past expenses, in the ledger and in the balance list of the remaining members, and drops out of every picker. Re-adding means clearing `removed_at`. · source: group-member-removal.md Contract/Decisions; baseline.sql:3295 column comment, 2026-10-26 · proposed → domain/glossary.md (with the entry above)
- A stranded balance (a removed member's balance moved after removal, e.g. an old expense was edited) gets no Pay action. The fix is "No longer in this group. Add them back to settle up." `pay_back` refuses a payer or payee who is not active. · source: lib/l10n/app_en.arb `paymentStrandedHint`; 20261026120000 `pay_back` :63/:74, 2026-10-26 · not proposed (an app fact, already in code)
- With a `RETURNING` clause, every newly inserted or updated row must satisfy the table's SELECT policies, or the statement errors. So `save_group_all`'s `insert into "group" … returning id` needs the creator SELECT branch before the creator has a member row. · source: https://www.postgresql.org/docs/15/sql-createpolicy.html, 2026-10-26 · proposed → domain/postgres-rls.md
- An UPDATE that reads columns (WHERE, RETURNING, the SET right-hand side) is also filtered by the SELECT policies, on both the existing and the new row. WITH CHECK sees only the proposed new row, never the old values. So a policy cannot say "own row: is_favorite yes, removed_at no", because column grants are per column, not per row. · source: same page, 2026-10-26 · proposed → domain/postgres-rls.md

## Open
- Product ruling: what does a soft-removed user see? Does the group vanish from their list (it does today for hard removal)? Do they keep a read-only view of their own history or debts, their statistics, and friend balances from that group? A stranded balance would then be invisible to the person who owes it. No plan states it. group-member-removal speaks only for the remaining members
- Re-join: should a soft-removed member be able to rejoin by themselves with a still-valid old link? join_group allows it today. Or must a member reset the link or add them back? Should removal rotate `invite_token`?
- The creator: should `group.user_id` stay "last editor" (save_group_all overwrites it), or become the real creator, or be dropped from access entirely? Candidates for keeping group create working: the creator branch only while the group has no member rows (a definer helper, since a sub-select inside a group_member policy recurses), or a DEFINER create path. Which one is the planner's call; whether the creator is special is Jakob's
- Own-row UPDATE: drop the branch (active members are covered by `is_group_member`), or restrict it to `removed_at is null` in USING and WITH CHECK. Either way, released builds' client-side re-join (update own `removed_at = null`) is refused, the same hard-fail as group-id-leak. Unverified whether any released build still sends it
- Pre-existing, not a removed-member issue: any active member can set `group.user_id` to themselves (GRANT ALL, no column grant on "group"). Before being removed, that plants the creator backdoor above. In scope or a separate row?
- Realtime: an open screen on a removed member's device gets no event saying "you were removed". Is a stale view until the next fetch acceptable?
